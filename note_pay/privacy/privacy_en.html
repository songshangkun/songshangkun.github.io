NotePay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04 · Effective: 2026-09-04

1. Who we are

NotePay ("we") is a local-first expense-tracking app. This policy explains how we handle your information. For questions, contact privacy@notepay.app.

2. Information we collect

NotePay is built local-first: your ledger data is stored on your device (on-device SQLite) by default. We do not operate a backend account system and do not upload your plaintext ledger to our servers without your consent.

  • Entries you create: amounts, categories, notes, dates, place names — stored locally only.
  • Location (optional): only when you use map pin-drop, we call AMap (Gaode) to resolve the place name you select; we do not track your location in the background.
  • Camera/Photo (optional): only when you use photo receipt recognition, images are read temporarily for on-device/edge OCR; original images are not retained on our servers.
  • Encrypted LLM key (local only): when you configure AI features, your API key is stored encrypted on your device only; we cannot read it.
  • AI-processed content: in remote mode, the text and receipt images you submit are sent to the LLM endpoint you configure (e.g. ModelScope / OpenAI-compatible); in on-device mode they are processed locally and never leave the device.

3. How we use information

  • To generate bills, statistics, charts and reminders on your device;
  • To call map/camera/LLM only with your authorization;
  • We do not sell your ledger or use it for behavioral advertising.

4. Sharing with third parties

  • AMap (Gaode): receives only the coordinate/place you select, for reverse-geocoding the place name.
  • Your configured LLM provider: AI data is handled under its own privacy policy; please review it.
  • Ads (Android free tier only): the free tier may show third-party rewarded video ads; the ad network does not see your ledger content.
  • The iOS version contains no ad SDK.

5. Data storage & retention

Your ledger stays on your device until you delete records or uninstall the app. We keep no server-side copy and cannot recover your data remotely. Please back up your data yourself.

6. Your rights

6.1 European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. The data controller is NotePay; you may contact us at the email above. Where we rely on your consent (e.g. location, camera, LLM key), you may withdraw it at any time.

6.2 United Kingdom (UK GDPR)

If you are in the UK, the same rights as under GDPR apply, with the UK supervisory authority (ICO) as the lead authority.

6.3 United States – California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. NotePay does not sell personal information. You may request access to or deletion of your data; since data is local-only, you manage or delete it directly in the app.

6.4 China (PIPL)

If you are in mainland China, you have rights to access, copy, correct, delete, and withdraw consent under the PIPL. Data is local-only; withdrawal of consent is equivalent to uninstalling the app.

6.5 Other regions

Japan (APPI), Korea (PIPA), Singapore (PDPA) and others: applicable local rights apply; contact us for assistance.

7. Children

NotePay is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). Any child's data stays on their device; we perform no backend collection.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in-app or via this page. Continued use means acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Privacy: privacy@notepay.app
