Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase (Pro / lifetime unlock)?

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. The unlock is tied to your App Store account, so it re-activates automatically on any device where you sign in with the same Apple ID. No account or password is required.

How do I export or back up my data?

Your ledger lives on your device. Use Settings → Export to save a copy (e.g. CSV / JSON), and the in-app transfer / sync feature to move data between books. We do not store your data on a server.

Is my data private? Do you show ads?

Yes — privacy is the core design. NotePay is local-first with no account system: your entries are processed on-device and are not uploaded to a cloud we control. The iOS version contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The AI assistant is not responding / returns an error.

Check your network connection and the LLM configuration under Settings. If you use the on-device model, make sure the language model is downloaded. Still stuck? Email us with your device model and iOS version.
