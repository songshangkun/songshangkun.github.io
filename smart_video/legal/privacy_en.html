2. The short version

  • Your media library, subtitles, notes and study records live on your device. We run no account system and no backend that stores your study data.
  • We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for behavioural advertising.
  • This version of the App contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK.
  • Photo library and file access are optional and used only when you import or export media. We never access the camera, microphone or your location.
  • AI features send text you select to an LLM endpoint you configure yourself — see section 4. On-device speech recognition never leaves your device.

3. Information we handle

Because the App is local-first, most information never leaves your device.

3.1 Information stored on your device

What Why Where it stays
Media library entries: file references, names, imported URLs / HLS links, playback position To let you reopen and resume your study media On-device database (app sandbox)
Subtitle files and transcription cache To display subtitles and enable word lookup On-device database
AI explanation & summary cache (words, sentences, media summaries) To show prior results without re-calling the AI On-device database
Word / flashcard study records and review schedules To drive spaced-repetition review On-device database
Notes you create To keep your study notes On-device (memory / local store)
App settings, including theme, language and subtitle style To remember your preferences On-device preferences
LLM endpoint address and key, if you configure one To call the AI features you enable On-device, encrypted; we cannot read it

We hold no server-side copy of the above and cannot restore it for you. Please keep your own backups.

3.2 Information handled only when you use a specific feature

  • Photo Library (optional). When you import media, the App reads videos / audio you select from your Photo Library. When you export study material, the App writes to your Photo Library. We never scan your library in the background. You may decline the permission; the rest of the App keeps working.
  • Files / Documents (optional). When you import local media or documents, the App reads the files you pick from the Files app / On My iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to a server we operate.
  • No microphone, no camera, no location. Smart Video does not request or use the microphone, camera or location services. Speech recognition runs on audio already in your imported media file using on-device models — it never records from the microphone.

3.3 Information sent outside your device

Only in these cases:

  1. AI processing. When you use AI features (subtitle translation, word & sentence explanation, summary & follow-up Q&A), the text you select is sent to the LLM endpoint you configure yourself in Settings → Model Library → LLM Server. The recipient is the provider you choose; we do not operate it, cannot read the address or key you configure, and keep no copy of what you submit. Please review that provider's privacy policy.
  2. Speech recognition (on-device). Transcription runs locally with sherpa models you download in Model Library. Audio and text are not sent anywhere.
  3. Purchase verification. In-app purchases are verified by the app store on your device (currently the Apple App Store). We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.
  4. Remote configuration and model downloads. The App fetches configuration files and on-device ASR (sherpa) models from the developer's GitHub Pages / GitHub. These requests do not carry your media or study content.

We do not operate advertising or analytics SDKs, so no identifier is shared with advertising networks.

4. AI features, in plain words

Smart Video offers two kinds of AI processing:

  • On-device speech recognition (ASR): transcription runs on your device with a sherpa model you download. Your audio and text never leave your device — nothing is transmitted.
  • User-configured LLM endpoint (BYOK): translation, explanation and summary are produced by sending the selected text to the LLM address and key you enter yourself in Settings → Model Library → LLM Server. The provider is chosen by you — we neither operate it nor can we read the address or key you configure. Please review that provider's privacy and retention policy, and do not submit information you consider confidential through this channel.

You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings; the player, subtitles and local study tools keep working without AI.

5. Why we process your information

We process information only to: import and play your media; display subtitles and enable word lookup; run on-device transcription you request; call the AI endpoint you configure; verify purchases; and keep the App working and secure.

We do not use your study content to train models, to profile you, or to target advertising.

7. How long we keep information

Your library, subtitles, notes and study records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the data, or uninstall the App. Deleting or uninstalling removes them permanently; we keep no copy and cannot recover them.

Content you submit to your configured LLM provider is retained according to that provider's retention policy.

8. Security

Your data is stored in a local database protected by your device's own security (device passcode, disk encryption). The LLM endpoint address and key are stored encrypted on the device and are never transmitted to us. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please protect your device and keep backups.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction (13 in the United States, 16 in much of the EEA, and as defined locally elsewhere). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.
