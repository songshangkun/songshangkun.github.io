Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase (lifetime unlock)?

Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases. The lifetime unlock is tied to your App Store account, so it re-activates automatically on any device where you sign in with the same Apple ID. No account or password is required.

How do I import media to study?

Smart Video can play media from three sources: Files on your device, your Photo Library, and a remote URL / HLS stream you paste in. Imported media stays on your device; we never upload it to a server.

Is my data private? Do you show ads?

Yes — privacy is the core design. Smart Video is local-first with no account system: your media library, subtitles, notes and study records are stored on your device and are not uploaded to a cloud we control. The iOS version contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The AI features (translation / explanation / summary) are not responding.

AI features send the selected text to the LLM endpoint you configure yourself in Settings → Model Library → LLM Server. Please check that the address and key are correct and that your device has network access. We do not operate that endpoint and cannot read what you configure. Still stuck? Email us with your device model and iOS version.

Transcription / subtitles are not generated.

On-device speech recognition uses sherpa models you download once in Settings → Model Library. After a model is installed, transcription runs entirely on your device and nothing is sent to a server. Make sure the media already has (or can produce) a transcript.
